Why We Need a TransactionAttempt Ledger

The order state machine knows the business stage. It does not reliably know every transaction we tried. The ledger adds that missing delivery memory so recovery and future gas bumping can reason from durable evidence.

Expected move 6 → 7/10

After: Delivery Has Its Own Durable Memory

1
Persist planned attempt before broadcast

Order id, tx type, chain, signer, nonce, fees, and tx payload are saved.

↓
2
Attach hash after RPC accepts

The attempt becomes broadcast and can be queried by order, stage, nonce, status, or hash.

↓
3
Monitor updates the attempt

Confirmed, reverted, indeterminate, or submit-rejected outcomes are recorded.

↓
4
Recovery can restore missing order hashes

If the order field is missing, recovery uses the ledger as fallback evidence.

What the Ledger Adds

Order State

Business truth: Executing, Executed, Settled, Finalized. This stays clean.

OIF Contracts

On-chain truth: duplicate fill or claim is rejected by idempotency.

TransactionAttempt Ledger

Stage Nonce Hash Status
Fill 42 0xf1... broadcast
Fill 42 0xa8... indeterminate
Claim 91 0xc3... confirmed

Recovery

Looks up attempts when the order's hash field is missing or stale.

Future Bumping

Replacement txs can link old attempt to new attempt instead of blind retrying.

The Lifecycle We Are Adding

planned

Saved after nonce and fee policy are known, before network broadcast.

broadcast

RPC accepted and returned a tx hash. The tx may still be pending.

confirmed

Receipt succeeded. Recovery can safely advance the business stage.

indeterminate

RPC or monitor could not decide. Recovery should check again later.

replacement ready

Later PR: same nonce, bumped gas, linked via replacement_of/replaced_by.

Failures This Fix Reduces

Crash After Broadcast

Today the order may never store the hash. The ledger preserves the attempt and nonce.

RPC Ambiguity

Timeouts become indeterminate attempts instead of lost context.

Nonce Drift

We can see which nonce was used for each tx stage before deciding what to retry.

Stuck Claim Path

If fill happened but local state is incomplete, recovery has a better route to continue toward claim.

What We Are Not Fixing Yet

This is the foundation. Same-nonce gas bumping, raw signed transaction rebroadcast, OIF idempotent revert decoding, and chain log scanners should come next. Without the ledger, those features would still be guessing from incomplete local state.
